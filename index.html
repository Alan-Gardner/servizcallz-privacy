Serviz Callz: privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Serviz Callz is a work app for field engineers. It lets an engineer receive their jobs, record their progress, and complete jobs on their phone. It is published by Alan Gardner. For any question about privacy, e-mail servizcallz@gmail.com.

The app is only used by engineers who have been given a login by the company they work for (called "your employer" below). That company decides what job information is kept and for how long.

What the app sends, and where it goes

The app only sends information to your employer's own Serviz Callz server, which records it in your employer's job system. This includes:

  • your engineer code and name
  • job status updates (accepted, on route, on site) and the times you recorded them
  • work notes, parts used and form answers
  • photos and files you take or choose for a job
  • the customer's name and signature, and your own signature, when you complete a job
  • follow-ups, no-access reasons and out-of-hours job requests
  • a notification ID, so the server can tell your phone about new jobs

The app does not collect your location, contacts, calendar, messages or browsing history, and it contains no advertising.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics. A crash report contains the error, the app version, and the phone's model and operating system. It contains no customer or job details and does not identify you.

Nothing is shared or sold

No information is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else. Google processes crash reports and delivers notifications on our behalf only.

How your information is kept secure

  • Everything the app sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Your login is kept in your phone's secure storage.
  • The app's job data is left out of phone backups.

How long it is kept

  • On your phone: completed jobs and their photos are removed after the period chosen in the app's settings (3 to 30 days), once everything has been sent.
  • On the server and in your employer's job system: records are kept as your employer's normal job records, under your employer's own policies.
  • Crash reports: deleted by Firebase after 90 days.

Accounts and deleting your data

You cannot create an account in the app. Engineer accounts are created and removed by your employer's office in their job system. To have your account and its data removed, contact your employer's office, or e-mail servizcallz@gmail.com and we will pass your request on.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
